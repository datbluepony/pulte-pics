Verdana Village · How it works

From your starred map to finished photos, the same day.

Every other Sunday you star the lots you need. This is the system Andrew built to read that map, plan the drive, shoot every house and deliver full-resolution photos to you, sorted by lot.

Step 1 · Your map

Your stars become a shot list in about a minute.

The app opens the PDF you send and finds every star: its exact position and its colour, which tells it whose lot it is. It lines each map screenshot up against the master site map and reads the lot number under the star.

  • Every lot number is checked against all 2,400 lots on the master map.
  • A star that sits between two lots, or just off the edge of a screenshot, is flagged for a second look.
  • Andrew sees a snapshot of each star beside the lot number before anything is final.
Step 2 · The route

The fastest way through the community, on real streets.

The route is planned over the actual road layout of Verdana Village, around the lakes and preserves, so the stops come in the order you would drive them. The site map is pinned to real GPS coordinates, so the app always knows where he is.

Step 3 · On site

A live map walks him to each house.

The next lot is circled on a live map with his position, the direction he is facing and the road path to it. When he arrives, the screen turns green.

  • A rapid camera saves a photo on every tap, always landscape.
  • For low light, the iPhone camera with Night mode is one tap away.
  • Pick the construction stage and the customer note writes itself.
Step 4 · Delivered

Uploaded before he reaches the next house.

Photos go up in the background at full resolution, two at a time, and each one gets a green tick once it is safely stored. When the last lot is in, you get an email that the run is complete.

Your side

Everything you need, sorted by lot.

Your page shows every home you have in the run, its stage, a customer-ready note and the full-resolution photos. Mark each update sent and watch your ring fill up.
